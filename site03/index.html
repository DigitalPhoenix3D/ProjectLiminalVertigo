The Architecture of Fear

Project Liminal Vertigo is a first-person, chase-based psychological horror game set within vast, procedurally shifting liminal environments.

Players must navigate endless corridors, surreal spaces, and interconnected structures while being hunted by unseen or partially seen entities. The game emphasizes tension, spatial awareness, and survival through movement rather than combat.

Every decision—every turn—can mean escape or encounter.

Emergent Despair

“In Project Liminal Vertigo, seekers are dropped into unpredictable environments where the only constant is pursuit. There are no strict rules on how to survive — seekers may choose to run, hide, loop corridors, or attempt to outmaneuver the entities in their own way. The experience is driven by emergent gameplay. Each seeker develops their own strategies, routes, and instincts over time. The Cosmic Cards are a dim light in the vast chaotic darkness. But beware, seekers stick out like a sore thumb when they add the light of the cards to their own.”

Beyond these dark hallways lies something far older.

The environments are not random. They are fragments of a world shaped by ancient forces — remnants of a forgotten conflict between incomprehensible entities.

Some believe the structures are growing. Others believe they are remembering.

Whatever the truth, something is watching. Waiting.

And the deeper you go, the more it fractures your mind, body, and soul.

Developed By

Digital Phoenix Studio

Digital Phoenix Studio is an independent solo game development effort focused on creating immersive, atmospheric experiences that blend gameplay systems with deeper philosophical and existential themes.

Project Liminal Vertigo is the studio's flagship project, exploring the intersection of procedural generation, horror design, and emergent player-driven experiences.
