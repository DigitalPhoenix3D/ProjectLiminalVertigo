01 — Gameplay

Run. Hide. Or simply listen to the walls breathe.

Every Rift is procedurally generated with ordered chaos. No two runs share the same geometry. Play it slow and survive by stealth — or sprint until your lungs burn and the monster's presence looms ever closer behind you.

// 001

Procedural Hallways

Modular tile generation produces corridors and rooms that rearrange every run. The map you memorized last night is gone.

// 002

Intelligent Hunters

Monsters listen, learn, and predict. A panic sprint will be heard. So will the breath you're trying to hold.

// 003

Cosmic Cards

Bend reality with rare cards drawn from the Cosmic Universe — its unbreakable tether to your soul snaps you out of the Eldritch Spiral's grip but not for long. Each card costs something.

// 004

Stealth or Sprint

Lurk silently toward the cards, or detonate into a full chase sequence. The Rifts accommodate cowards and lunatics alike.

02 — The Rifts

Four wrong places.
None of them want you to leave.

Each Rift is a separate wound in reality, stitched from the memory of a place that may have existed once.

Rift I

The Spaceship

Cold-welded corridors of a ship that forgot its destination. Atmosphere thin. Lights, intermittent.

Rift II

The Dungeon

Wet stone and unfinished prayers. Something down here was buried while it was still moving.

Rift III

The Foundry

An industrial complex that never stopped churning. The machines remember their old shifts.

Rift IV

The Mansion

Victorian wallpaper peeling like skin. Every mirror is a door, and they have all been left open.

04 — Developer

Digital Phoenix Studio

Φ

One developer. One obsession.

Project Liminal Vertigo is a solo passion project. Designed, built, and realized by an independent, singular developer in Unreal Engine 5, using only Blueprints. The goal: to prove the utility of AI assistance in developing their first game as an atmospheric horror with the kind of deep, cross-game lore most studios are too scared to commit to.

This is the first wound in a much larger universe. More games. Same Void.
